Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I register for StudentHub?

Open the Register page, complete the required information, choose a username, and create a strong password. Practical 9 checks the form on the server and stores a password hash in MySQL.

2. How do I log in?

Use the Login page with your username or email. Practical 10 starts a secure session and sends students and administrators to their role-protected dashboards.

3. Can I update my profile on this website?

The Profile page is a read-only sample layout. Editing and saving profile information are not available in this static HTML practical.

4. Where can I see upcoming events?

Visit the Events page to see sample event listings. Confirm actual dates, locations, and registration details through official college announcements.

5. Are the student and event details real?

No. Profile details are placeholders, and event information is illustrative or unconfirmed. Do not treat this page as an official student record or college schedule.

6. Does StudentHub store information I enter?

Registration creates an account in the local practical database, and sign-in uses sessions. Demo credentials are provided in Practical 10 documentation; do not use a personal password. The Contact page remains a local form-processing demonstration.

7. How can I contact the support team?

See the Contact page for the support layout. Its contact details are placeholders and must be verified with the college before use.

8. What should I do if I find a problem?

You can review the Feedback page to see how suggestions could be collected. The feedback form is not connected and does not submit responses.
